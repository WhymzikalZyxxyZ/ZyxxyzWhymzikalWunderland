Understanding the Numbers
BMI Categories
  • Under 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5–24.9  — Healthy weight
  • 25.0–29.9  — Overweight
  • 30.0+      — Obese (Class I–III)

BMI doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution — use it as one data point, not a diagnosis.

TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier
  • BMR — calories burned at complete rest
  • TDEE — total daily energy expenditure
  • Deficit of ~500 cal/day ≈ ~1 lb/week loss
  • Surplus of ~250–500 cal/day for muscle gain

Calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most validated BMR formula for general populations.
